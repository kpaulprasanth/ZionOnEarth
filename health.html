Divine Health & Vitality

The Incorruptible Stream of Wholeness

The Law of the Spirit of Life

Divine healing is not an occasional emergency visit from God—it is the perpetual, invigorating flow of Zoe operating throughout every cell, organ, and nerve. In Zion, sickness has no lawful right of habitation.

"Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we esteemed Him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. But He was wounded for our transgressions, He was bruised for our iniquities; the chastisement for our peace was upon Him, and by His stripes we are healed." — Isaiah 53:4–5

The Word of God is active biological medicine, rejuvenating our spirit, soul, and mortal bodies.

"My son, give attention to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. Do not let them depart from your eyes; keep them in the midst of your heart; for they are life to those who find them, and health to all their flesh." — Proverbs 4:20–22
"Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers." — 3 John 1:2
"Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all His benefits: who forgives all your iniquities, who heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with lovingkindness and tender mercies." — Psalm 103:2–4
"But if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, He who raised Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who dwells in you." — Romans 8:11
