Liberty Through Resurrection Life

Deliverance is not lifelong symptom-management; it is total liberation from the root spiritual bonds of fear, trauma, and compulsion through the power of Christ.

"Therefore if the Son makes you free, you shall be free indeed." — John 8:36

The divine life within you is stronger than any generational pattern or chemical dependency.

"Stand fast therefore in the liberty by which Christ has made us free, and do not be entangled again with a yoke of bondage." — Galatians 5:1
"He has delivered us from the power of darkness and conveyed us into the kingdom of the Son of His love, in whom we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins." — Colossians 1:13–14
"The Spirit of the Lord God is upon Me, because the Lord has anointed Me to preach good tidings to the poor; He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to those who are bound." — Isaiah 61:1
"For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has made me free from the law of sin and death." — Romans 8:2
