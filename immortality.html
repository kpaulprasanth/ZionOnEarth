The Revelation of Immortality

Life and Incorruption Brought to Light

The Eternal Blueprint of Zoe

In biblical theology, immortality is not merely endless existence—it is the direct impartation of God's uncreated, incorruptible life (Zoe) swallowing up mortality. Christ has abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the Gospel.

When believers awaken to their union with Christ, they walk no longer under the dominion of corruption, decay, and fear, but under the law of the Spirit of Life in Christ Jesus.

"Our Savior Jesus Christ, who has destroyed death and has brought life and immortality to light through the gospel." — 2 Timothy 1:10

As Zion is established on Earth, we contend for the full manifestation of this reality: spiritual vitality, divine endurance, and unbroken fellowship with the Father.

Teaching • Zoe Life Series 1. Swallowing Up Mortality in Victory

In 1 Corinthians 15, the Apostle Paul reveals that corruption cannot inherit incorruption. The physical body and mind are meant to be clothed upon by the Spirit of Life.

This victory does not begin at the grave—it begins the moment the seed of Zoe is deposited into the spirit of the believer. As we renew our minds to this divine blueprint, fear of decay and mortality loses its grip, releasing peace and supernatural health into our daily walk.

Revelation • Kingdom Studies 2. The Tree of Life vs. The Tree of Knowledge

From the beginning in Genesis, two sources were presented to humanity: the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil (which produced legalism, striving, and mortality) and the Tree of Life (which produced eternal communion and Zoe).

Living under Zion means feeding continuously from Christ as the Tree of Life. We no longer define our spiritual state by human effort, but by resting in His finished incorruptible work.
